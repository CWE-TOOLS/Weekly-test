Not yet scheduled Castings a PM marked "Ready for scheduling" on the portal's Optimizer Hours tab, that have a Casting Inventory and no days on the grid yet. Hours come from the Optimizer Hours tab. Schedule = spread those tasks from the cast date.
How the spread works The Staging tab sets each casting's days and the task of every day; the Optimizer Hours tab adds the hours per day. The scheduler lays those days out as they are, so the grid always totals exactly the hours entered. A casting still on its previous task list is spread over its staging days instead. Build Crate always lands on a Monday (move it by hand if it must be elsewhere). The Cast team lands on the cast date; earlier phases run back from it, later ones forward. Weekends are skipped unless you allow one; holidays are never used.
